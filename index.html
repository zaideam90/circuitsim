Shortcuts

Z wire tool — R rotate — Delete delete — Esc cancel
F flip wire bend — Space run/stop
Right-click: finish the current wire, right-click again to return to Select. Wheel: zoom — middle button: pan
Wires: drag the middle of a wire to move it, or drag an end to reconnect it.
While running: click buttons/breakers to operate them, click a wire to cut or reconnect it, click a faulted drive or DC supply to reset it.
